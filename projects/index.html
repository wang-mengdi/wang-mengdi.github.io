Shared simulation infrastructure

SimpleX

A C++ framework for physical simulation, optimisation and visualisation, supporting research across different representations and numerical methods.

Shared geometry, discretisation, solver and visualisation components support the construction of different physical simulators.

GPU numerical computing

MESO

A C++ and CUDA framework that turns numerical methods into reusable GPU components for physical simulation and computational design.

MESO development began in 2022 under the leadership of Mengdi Wang. Reusable data structures, discrete differential operators and GPU solvers support numerical algorithm development.

GPU multigrid solvers

Cirrus

A GPU codebase for adaptive fluid simulation, with particle–grid flow maps and multigrid solvers.

Cirrus development began in 2024. The simulator was developed by Mengdi Wang. It combines particle trackers, adaptive grids and multigrid solvers for GPU fluid simulation.

Cirrus and Cirrus-AMG

Cirrus-AMG builds on Cirrus and provides the implementation accompanying the arXiv paper on algebraically consistent coarsening.

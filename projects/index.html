SimpleX

SimpleX is a simulation framework used to study vortices beneath a free surface and flow within thin liquid films.

I took over maintenance and development of SimpleX at Dartmouth and continued that work at Georgia Tech. Source code

MESO

MESO is a GPU library for tracking material interfaces and for computational design.

I led the team that started MESO in 2022. We used it in our work on interface tracking. Other groups have used it for fluidic design and thin-shell optimisation. Source code

Journal of Computational Physics · 2025

Triangle Edge Cuts

The method tracks an interface as it crosses the edges of a triangle mesh while conserving the volume of each material.

I began building Cirrus on MESO in 2024.

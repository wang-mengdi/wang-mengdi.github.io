    \section{Research Summary}

        \begin{onecolentry}
            %My research develops scalable numerical algorithms and GPU-based systems for multiscale physical simulation, focusing on unified frameworks that integrate representation, transport, and solver design, with emerging directions in AI-assisted and physics-embedded modeling for adaptive and interactive simulation.\\
            My research develops multiscale representations, interface and transport algorithms, and adaptive GPU solvers for physical simulation, while exploring simulation as a two-way interface between AI and the real world: AI expands what can be simulated, and physics provides testable principles for reliable, human-centered intelligent systems.\\
